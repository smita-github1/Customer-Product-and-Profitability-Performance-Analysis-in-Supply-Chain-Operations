#Project-Customer-Product-and-Profitability-Performance-Analysis-in-Supply-Chain-Operations

Detailed guide and project requirements for the Customer, Product, and Profitability Performance Analysis in Supply Chain Operations analysis.

#Background and Context
For a global logistics provider like APL Logistics:

• Not all customers are equally profitable

• High sales do not always mean high profit

• Discounts and shipping costs can silently erode margins

• Certain product categories or markets may be loss-making


#Leadership needs answers to:
Which customers, products, and regions truly generate value for the business?

This project delivers financial clarity and margin intelligence

Company Link-

https://www.apllogistics.com/

#Problem Statement

Despite having detailed order and sales data, the organization lacks

• Visibility into profitability by customer and product

• Understanding of discount-driven margin erosion

• Identification of high-value vs low-value customers

• Market and category-level profit diagnostics

As a result, pricing and customer strategies may be revenue-focused but profit-blind

#Analytical Methodology (Step-by-Step)

->Data Cleaning & Financial Validation

• Validate sales and profit fields

• Remove inconsistent or zero-value records

• Normalize currency-related values

->Revenue & Profit Overview

• Compute total revenue and profit

• Compare revenue vs profit trends

• Identify profit concentration patterns

->Product & Category Profitability Analysis

• Analyze profit and margin by:

Product Name

Category Name

• Identify high-revenue but low-margin products

• Detect loss-making categories

->Customer Contribution Analysis

• Aggregate sales and profit by Customer Id

• Identify:

     High-value customers

     Low-margin or loss-making customers
     
• Segment customers by value tiers

->Discount Impact Diagnostics

• Compare profit margins with and without discounts

• Analyze discount rate vs profit ratio

• Identify discount thresholds causing margin erosion

->Market & Regional Profit Analysis

• Compare profitability across:

    Markets
    Order Regions
    Countries
    
• Identify markets with strong revenue but weak profit
